    Where NASA Data Becomes a Walking Plan.

    • 01

      Planning Limits Are Not Safety Limits

      Every status reads Within Planning Limits, Review Required or Exceeds Planning Limit. Nothing is ever called safe.

    • 02

      Missing Data Stays Missing

      Where there is no evidence, the console shows n/a or Unavailable instead of inventing a value.

    • 03

      AI May Explain, Never Invent

      A deterministic engine owns every route and number. answers through and labels each answer as AI interpretation.
